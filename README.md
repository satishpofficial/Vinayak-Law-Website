# Lawyer Simple — customizable website

A responsive, original HTML/CSS/JavaScript starter inspired by the broad professional style and section structure of the Wix Lawyer (Simple) template. It is not Wix's source code.

## Run locally
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser, or serve the folder:
   `python3 -m http.server 8000`
3. Visit `http://localhost:8000`.

## Customize
- Replace the sample name, title, bio, experience, services, contact information, and quote in `index.html`.
- Change colors and typography in the `:root` section of `styles.css`.
- Replace the Unsplash background URLs in `styles.css` with images you have rights to use.
- Change `hello@example.com` in `index.html` and `script.js` to your contact email.
- The contact form uses a `mailto:` link; it does not securely send/store submissions. For a production form, connect a backend or form provider.
- The sample legal copy and service descriptions are placeholders; replace them with accurate details for your practice.

No build tools or dependencies are required. Google Fonts and the sample background photos require an internet connection.
